About YVGA

A global platform for youth voices

YVGA aims to bring together emerging leaders, students, changemakers and young professionals for thoughtful conversations about issues shaping their generation.

Youth Leadership

Develop leadership skills, confidence and responsible global citizenship.

Global Dialogue

Exchange ideas across cultures, communities and countries.

Collaboration

Build connections that can lead to practical youth-led initiatives.

Shared Future

Explore constructive solutions to challenges affecting young people worldwide.
